Can You Turn a Smooth Bowl of Hot Pap?

Turning raw fermented akamu / ogi into thick, glossy custard is all in the wrist, water ratio, and timing:

1
Thick Slurry

Add just enough cool water to crush every lump. Too much cool water kills the heat!

2
100°C Boil & Catch

Wait for a rolling boil. Pour hot water steadily—don't get fooled by the 1-second lag!

3
Whip Fast Circles

Cut the pour right as it catches and stir briskly in circles for a velvety, lump-free shine!
